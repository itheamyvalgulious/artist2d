## 3. New findings by category

### 3.1 Tiles
- Complete and atlas-consistent (25/25 at declared slots, 32×32, opaque, no AA, no magenta). `vent`, `hazard`, `window`, `light_strip`, `pipe_*` all read as intended at 1×.
- **Deck family is not top-registered.** `deck_top` rows 0–11 are dark (78,78,99 only on row 0, then 54/20/8/35) and the bright 8 px surface band is at rows 12–19; `deck_fill` puts its bright band at rows 10–19. Consequently the room's walk line (y=512) is a 1 px seam followed by an **11 px dark recess**, and the visible lit bands fall at y=523–531, 555–564, 587–596, 619–628 → the deck reads as four stacked ledges, not one floor.
- **Accent strips are per-tile and get cut.** `wall_flat_light`/`wall_flat_violet` carry a vertical 3 px strip with bracket ticks at rows 6–7 and 22–23. Because the cluster logic re-assigns tiles inside 4 boxes, those strips appear as isolated 1–3 tile dashes with square ends, e.g. cyan 540…542 × 377…422, violet 566…569 × 297…342, cyan 582…585 × 249…282. Read as broken neon, and they are the same "run ends in mid-air" defect as round-1 #5, now vertical.
- **`light_strip` does not merge with `ceiling`.** It is pasted at row 1 (y=32…63) with a uniform `(35,35,46)` field, so each of the 5 strips replaces a 32 px slice of the ceiling's lit soffit (y=44…52, `(78,78,99)`) with a navy rectangle crossed by a cyan rule — a hard 32 px patch, visible in `05`/`06` across the top.
- **`grate_fill`/`grate_top` are 1 px dither noise** (10.0 % isolated single pixels vs 0.8 % for `deck_fill`), against the brief's "large crisp square pixel clusters"; at 1× they read as static with an X pattern.
- **Duplicates**: `wall_flat`≡`wall_plain` (md5 `832a9796`), `wall_flat_light`≡`wall_light` (`37b11759`), `wall_flat_violet`≡`wall_violet` (`3d929b5c`) — 22 unique designs sold as 25.

## 5. Overall verdict

**FAIL.**

Two blockers and eight majors remain. The hull rib is finally anchored (y=64…511) and no longer clipped by the canvas, the occluded window is gone, the reactor and viewport style breaks are genuinely repaired, and pipe runs now have terminators — so the set has materially improved. But the rib's two-module stack still puts a flared, bright 42 px shelf across all five ribs (round-1 #1 is not closed), `cargo_stack` is still a 3×3 wall-panel grid (round-1 #6 is not closed), and the room now has its own new structural problems: a floor that is not top-registered (lit band 11 px below the walk line, four stacked ledges), a reactor standing inside a 56 px empty black picture frame, accent strips broken into floating dashes, a hazard run chopped mid-stripe, a grate patch stuck over the deck face, and a wall that is still 77 % one identical tile with clusters that are both low-contrast and partly behind a rib. The showcase character is no longer inside the console, but it straddles the dais and reads as hovering. Pixel hygiene (palette purity, no AA/blur, no magenta in shipped assets, no alpha holes) passes; the failure is design, assembly and readability.

## 6. Summary

This is a cleaner build than v1 but still not shippable. The tile atlas is complete and consistent (25 slots, 22 unique designs), the palette and pixel discipline are intact, and the three mega elements individually read well — the viewport is now a proper armoured porthole and the reactor is hard-edged and symmetric. What still fails is the assembled room: the "ceiling-to-floor" rib is made of two identical end-capped 224 px modules, so every rib carries a flared double-flange shelf at y=263…304 and the rib at x=704 loses its foot behind the console dais; the deck's lit surface sits 11 px below the ground anchor, so every prop and both characters look perched on a lip above four stacked plinth bands; the reactor is mounted inside an empty 3 px black frame 56 px taller than the machine; wall accent strips are chopped into floating dashes, the hazard run is cut mid-stripe at x=1248, the grate is stuck over the deck face, and 77 % of the wall is a single byte-identical tile whose detail clusters are low-contrast and partly occluded. Prop-wise `console_bank` ships, `server_rack` is hollow and noisy at the base, and `cargo_stack` still reads as a wall panel rather than stacked crates. Fix the rib module (single 448 px piece), re-register the deck surface, clear the reactor mount and rebuild `cargo_stack`, then give the wall real, visible detail and true pipe/run terminations — the set can pass the next round.
